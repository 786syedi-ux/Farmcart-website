Questions kitchens ask us

How do I order wholesale vegetables from Farmcart?

Browse today's prices on the Store page, or message us on WhatsApp. Minimum order is 200 kg total. Order by 10 PM for delivery before your kitchen opens the next day.

What's the minimum order?

200 kg total per order, 10 kg minimum per individual product.

What are the delivery charges?

One flat fee within 20 km of the yard, regardless of order size.

How do I pay?

UPI, credit/debit card, or cash on delivery. COD is available without prepayment once you've completed one verified order.

What if I'm not happy with the quality?

For prepaid orders, you get a 2-hour inspection window after delivery to flag any issue. For COD orders, inspect before you pay.
